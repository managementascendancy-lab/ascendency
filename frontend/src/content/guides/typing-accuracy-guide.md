## The math behind the classification system

Every [hero classification](/ascendancy) on Ascendancy has independent minimum thresholds for WPM, accuracy and consistency — you don't get to average your way past a requirement. SOVEREIGN needs 96%+ accuracy no matter how fast you type. A blazing 140 WPM run at 85% accuracy won't classify anywhere near the top, because accuracy is the hard gate, not a bonus stat.

## Errors cost more time than they seem to

On most typing interfaces, a single mistake costs you the time to type the wrong character, notice it, and correct it — often 3-4 keystrokes of overhead for a 1-character error. Typing 10% faster while making 3x the errors is almost always a net loss in real elapsed time, even before it affects your accuracy score directly.

## Your hands already know what to do — get out of the way

Most accuracy problems aren't a knowledge problem, they're an interference problem. Your fingers usually know the correct motion; anxiety about speed causes you to rush the motion before it completes, or to glance at the keyboard and lose your place in the text. Trust the motion, don't out-think it mid-keystroke.

## Practice at "boring" speed on purpose

It feels unproductive to deliberately type slower than you're capable of, but it's one of the highest-leverage things you can do. Running simulations where your only goal is zero errors — regardless of how slow that makes you — retrains the underlying motor pattern to be accurate by default, not accurate by effort.

## Watch for fatigue-driven accuracy drops

Accuracy tends to degrade in the last third of longer simulations as finger fatigue sets in. If your accuracy consistently drops off in 120-second runs, that's a sign to build up endurance gradually with shorter, high-accuracy sessions before attempting longer ones.

## The takeaway

If you only optimize one metric on Ascendancy, optimize accuracy first. It's the foundation the other two — consistency and, eventually, speed — are built on. Check your current numbers on your [Ascendant Console](/profile) and see which one is actually holding your classification back.
